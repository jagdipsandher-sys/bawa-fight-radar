BAWA Privacy

BAWA Fight Radar is a static personal dashboard. It does not require an account and does not sell personal information.

Information kept on your device

Your tab order, chosen Food & Drinks starting area and filters, and items you deliberately save to My Radar are stored in your browser’s local storage. You can clear them by clearing this site’s browser data.

Location

The site asks for device location only after you tap Use Current Location. The coordinates are used in your browser to order nearby food results and are not sent to BAWA. Google receives the map and place search needed to provide those results under Google’s own privacy policy.

Google Maps and Places

The Food & Drinks explorer loads ratings, review counts, hours, photos and place details live from Google. BAWA does not retain that Google content. Only a Google Place ID may be kept on your device for a starting area you select or a place you deliberately save, so its current details can be requested again later.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Maps Terms.

Contact

Use the feedback link on the main dashboard for privacy questions or deletion help.
